💌 About TravelMate

Crafted with warm tea, pastel washi tapes, and wanderlust.

A Cozy Travel Journal for Gentle Explorers

Traditional trip planners feel like boring corporate spreadsheets. TravelMate was created to bring back the warm, tactile nostalgia of physical scrapbooking, polaroid photography, and handwritten notes.

Whether you’re hunting for hidden bakeries in Paris, quiet temples in Kyoto, or sandy coves in Bali, TravelMate keeps your mornings gentle and your plans organized.

🎨
Pastel Scrapbook Aesthetic

Hand-cut tape, polaroids, sticky notes, and cozy colors for cheerful journaling.

🔒
100% Private & Local

Runs entirely in your web browser. No trackers, no accounts, and no databases needed.

📱
Responsive On Every Device

Check off your packing list while packing your suitcase on your phone!
